Recipe #1

Pasta

  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 1-2 people
Ingredients Quantity
Pasta 150-200g
Water 1.5-2 liters
Salt 1 teaspoon
Olive oil 1-2 tablespoons
Garlic 1-2 cloves
Tomato sauce 3-4 tablespoons
Cheese 20-30g

Step 1: Boil water in a pot. Add a pinch of salt.

Step 2: Add pasta and cook according to the package (usually 8–10 minutes).

Step 3: Drain the water.

Step 4: Heat a pan, add olive oil or butter.

Step 5: Add chopped garlic and cook for ~30 seconds (skip if you want it plain).

Step 6:Add pasta to the pan.

Step 7:Add tomato sauce or ketchup, mix well.

Step 8:Cook for 1–2 minutes.

Step 9:Serve and add cheese if you like.

Recipe #2

Pizza

  • Time: 45 minutes
  • Serves: 2-3 people
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 2 cups
Warm water 3/4 cup
Dry yeast 1 teaspoon
Sugar 1 teaspoon
Salt 1 teaspoon
Olive oil 2 teaspoons
Tomato sauce 1/2 cup
Cheese 150-200g
Toppings

Step 1:Mix warm water, yeast, and sugar. Wait 5 minutes.

Step 2:Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix and knead into soft dough.

Step 3:Rest dough for 10 minutes.

Step 4:Roll out the dough and place on a baking tray.

Step 5:Spread tomato sauce.

Step 6:Add cheese and toppings.

Step 7:Bake at 220°C for 12–15 minutes until golden.

Recipe #3

Dumplings

  • Time: 40 minutes
  • Serves: 2-3 people
Ingredients Quantity
Flour 2 cups
Water 3/4 cup
Salt 1 and 1/2 teaspoon
Minced meat 200g
Onion 1
Black pepper 1/2 teaspoon

Step 1: Mix flour, salt, and water. Knead into firm dough. Rest 10 minutes.

Step 2: Mix minced meat, onion, salt, pepper, and water.

Step 3: Roll dough thin, cut small circles.

Step 4: Put 1 teaspoon filling in each circle.

Step 5: Fold and seal dumplings.

Step 6: Boil salted water.

Step 7: Cook dumplings until they float + 2–3 minutes.

Step 8: Serve with butter, sour cream, or yogurt.
